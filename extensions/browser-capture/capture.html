Save selected media

Desktop Chrome / Edge. Select one visible image or video, pair an account, then review in ayamai. Nothing uploads automatically.

Only capture files you own or have permission to save. Public sharing requires the app’s separate rights confirmation.

Supported: anonymous, CORS-readable HTTPS PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, MP4 and WebM, up to 20 MiB. No redirects, URL queries, protected players, login cookies, blobs, streams, or embedded frames. Use the app’s cloud import for Drive, Dropbox and MEGA.

Only this explicitly selected tab is inspected. No browsing history, page text, cookies or credentials are collected. The app origin permission lets us transfer the selected bytes to your app tab, not sign in on your behalf.
