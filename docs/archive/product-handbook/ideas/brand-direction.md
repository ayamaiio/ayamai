# Ayamai Brand Direction — Exploration

**Status:** Historical exploration; visual direction superseded by [North Star v1](../../docs/design/visual-identity.md)
**Owner approval:** The later North Star preservation request approves the visual direction recorded in the linked specification. Earlier undecided logo, palette and visual-system statements below are historical. Other exploratory ideas remain unapproved; no implementation or release is authorized here.
**Origin:** Ayamai Product Strategy brainstorm, October 2026.

This brief preserves the current brainstorm so it can inform landing-page exploration and future design work. It is a working hypothesis: ideas may change, be deferred, or be abandoned. Discussion here does not authorize implementation or convert preferences into requirements.

## Personality and audience

Explore a personality that feels futuristic, high-tech, cutting-edge, atmospheric, and understated. Ayamai should carry an open-source and hacker-culture sensibility and feel technically sophisticated. The imagined audience already wants the product; communication should help that person understand and use it rather than rely on conventional SaaS sales positioning.

## Creative inspiration

Neon Genesis Evangelion, particularly Rei Ayanami, is a creative reference for a quiet, pretty, atmospheric sensibility and a possible cool purple and blue color direction. This is inspiration, not a requirement to reproduce source material or adopt any particular visual treatment.

Occasional naming conventions, terminology, visual motifs, or easter eggs may reward people who recognize the references. They should remain legible and enjoyable for people who do not.

## Product expression

A primary dark-mode experience is a preference to explore. The identity should be distinctive while allowing the media to remain the focus.

Consider two related expressions:

- **Brand expression:** landing page, identity, and messaging can carry more of the atmosphere and distinctiveness.
- **Product expression:** the application should remain restrained and media-focused, with a shared sensibility that does not compete with the content.

Use the existing application's style and architecture as the starting point. Iterate on its visual presentation rather than assuming a rewrite; a larger redesign remains possible if exploration gives a clear reason.

## Communication

Communication is a major improvement priority. Explore language that is precise, natural, understated, useful, and quietly confident. Avoid hype, excessive enthusiasm, generic SaaS marketing, and engagement-bait.

A useful tonal example is: “Upload complete. Added to your library.”

Apply this direction across status messages, notifications, empty states, tooltips, errors, instructions, and other user-facing text. The goal is to communicate clearly with the intended user, not to persuade them with exaggerated claims.

## Ecosystem

Use this direction to inform landing-page exploration and a possible shared design language across Ayamai, Sachiel, and future products. Shared principles can create family resemblance while each product keeps room for its own expression.

## Open questions

The brainstorm has not decided the exact logo, palette values, typography, motifs, naming rules, voice guide, or final visual system. These remain open for exploration and explicit owner review.

## Process note

Keep this direction labeled **Exploring / preliminary** until the owner explicitly approves a promotion. Do not treat it as approved requirements, a committed roadmap, or authorization to redesign or implement. No Linear tickets are implied by this brief.
