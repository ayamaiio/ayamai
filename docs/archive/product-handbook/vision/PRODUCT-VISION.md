# Ayamai Product Vision

**Status:** Initial direction approved; details continue to evolve.
**Stage:** Private, personal-first product.

## Vision

Ayamai is a beautiful, fast, private-by-default personal media collection and discovery experience, initially optimized for its creator and potentially later shared with a small, trusted, invite-only community.

The product should make it enjoyable to collect, organize, discover, watch, and selectively share media. It should feel quiet, precise, Japanese-influenced, and personal—not like a SaaS pitch. The owner is the first user; build for his actual use before hypothetical growth.

## Current product context

Ayamai focuses on adult media, including hentai and 3D animation. Access is invite-only. The product supports personal collection and discovery, with controlled sharing and social capabilities.

## Primary product priorities

1. Personal enjoyment and usefulness.
2. Speed, reliability, and polish.
3. Excellent media consumption.
4. Frictionless collection, importing, and organization.
5. A beautiful, coherent experience.
6. Useful personalization and recommendations.
7. Private sharing with trusted people.
8. Sensible future extensibility without premature complexity.

## Product principles

- **Build for the current owner:** Optimize for actual use before hypothetical audiences.
- **Experience over feature count:** Prefer fewer exceptionally polished capabilities.
- **Performance is a feature:** Fast loading, responsive controls, and reliable playback matter.
- **Privacy is foundational:** Make visibility and data use understandable and controlled.
- **Keep it simple:** Avoid complexity without a demonstrated benefit.
- **Do not scale prematurely:** Preserve reasonable options for later without compromising the current experience.
- **Explicit decisions matter:** Ideas and recommendations do not become commitments without owner approval.

## Business direction

Ayamai is private and personal-first today. Premium positioning and a possible anonymous contribution are exploratory context, not approved business models. The app currently has no payment, subscription, payout, pay-per-view, or donation flow. Do not add monetization until the owner explicitly chooses it.

## Product experience hypothesis

The owner has begun exploring a complementary mobile and desktop experience:
- Mobile may emphasize discovery, scrolling, and immediate media consumption.
- Desktop may emphasize gathering, uploading, organizing, curating, and longer-form viewing.
- A hybrid desktop workspace may allow a persistent or prominent video experience alongside library and management activities.

**This is exploratory, not an approved feature specification or implementation requirement.** See `product/ideas/desktop-mobile-experiences.md`.

## Decision filter

For a proposed feature or change, ask:
1. Would the owner personally enjoy or benefit from this?
2. Does it improve collecting, organizing, discovering, or consuming media?
3. Is the complexity justified by the experience?
4. What privacy, safety, reliability, and maintenance costs does it introduce?
5. Does it preserve useful future flexibility without overengineering today?

## Non-goals for the current stage

- Mass-market growth or venture-scale optimization.
- Building for large teams or complex organizational workflows.
- Premature infrastructure scaling.
- Monetization before the owner is satisfied with the core experience.
- Treating every possible feature as a roadmap commitment.
